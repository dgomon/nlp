    \section*{Question 4}
    \subsection*{a}
    Defining the cost of choosing a word $w_i$ as $c(w_i) =
        -\log P(w_i) = \log \frac{1}{P(w_i)}$, where $c(w_i) \ge 0$ since $0 < P(w_i) \le 1$,
        the problem is equivalent to minimizing the total cost $\sum_{i=1}^k c(w_i)$:
    \begin{align*}
        \argmax_{w_1, \dots, w_k} \prod_{i=1}^k P(w_i)
        &= \argmax_{w_1, \dots, w_k} (\log \prod_{i = 1}^{k}P(w_i))
            && \text{($a > b \iff \log a > \log b$)} \\
        &= \argmax_{w_1, \dots, w_k} \sum_{i=1}^k \log P(w_i)
            && (\log(ab) = \log(a) + \log(b)) \\
        &= \argmin_{w_1, \dots, w_k} \sum_{i=1}^k -\log P(w_i) \\
        &= \argmin_{w_1, \dots, w_k} \sum_{i=1}^k \log \frac{1}{P(w_i)}
            && (-\log(a) = \log(\frac{1}{a})) \\
        &= \argmin_{w_1, \dots, w_k} \sum_{i=1}^k c(w_i)
    \end{align*}

    \subsection*{b}
    \begin{tabbing}
    \\
    \textbf{function} \textsc{Min-Cost}($S, V$) \textbf{returns} \textit{min-cost} \\
    \quad $length \leftarrow \textsc{Length}(S)$ \\
    \quad Create a cost vector $A[length + 1]$ \\
    \quad \textit{$A[j]$ - minimum cost of segmenting the prefix of length $j$} \\
    \\
    \quad \# \textit{Initialization} \\
    \quad $A[0] = 0$ \\
    \quad $A[1 .. length] = \infty$ \\
    \\
    1. \quad \textbf{for} each starting word index $i$ \textbf{from} 0 \textbf{to} $length - 1$ \textbf{do} \\
    2. \quad\quad \textbf{if} $A[i] \neq \infty$ \\
    3. \quad\quad\quad \# \textit{letter $i$ is reachable from beginning of the word} \\
    4. \quad\quad\quad \textbf{for} each ending word index $j$ \textbf{from} i + 1 \textbf{to} $length$ \textbf{do} \\
    5. \quad\quad\quad\quad \textbf{if} $S[i + 1 \dots j] \in V$ \\
    6. \quad\quad\quad\quad\quad $A[j] = \min(A[j], A[i] - \log(P(S[i + 1 \dots j])))$
    \\
    \\
    \quad \# \textit{Termination} \\
    \quad \textbf{return} $A[length]$
    \end{tabbing}
    \subsection*{c}
    We keep an additional array \texttt{B} of \texttt{length + 1} elements.
    We can construct B by replacing step 6. with steps:
    \begin{tabbing}
        \quad \texttt{if (A[i] - log(P((S[i + 1 ... j]))) < A[j]):} \\
        \quad\quad \texttt{A[j] = A[i] - log(P((S[i + 1 ... j])))} \\
        \quad\quad \texttt{B[j] = i + 1}
    \end{tabbing}
    Once B has been constructed, starting from j = length, B[j] will contain the start index of the word that ends in index j.
    Backtracking from the last word, we thus can collect all the sub-words that minimize the cost.
    Algorithm to get the optimal division:
    \begin{tabbing}
        \quad Initialize an empty list of words $W = []$ and an index pointer $k = length$. \\
        \quad While $k > 0$: \\
        \quad\quad Let $start = B[k]$. \\
        \quad\quad Prepend the substring $S[start \dots k]$ to $W$. \\
        \quad\quad Update $k = start - 1$. \\
        \quad Return $W$ (reversed if appended).
    \end{tabbing}

    \subsection*{d}
    Outer loop has length n in Step 1 has length n. Inner loop (step 4) also has length n.
    Since it's given that complexity of lookup (step 5) is O(1) and also assuming O(1) complexity of assignment, lookup and
    log calculation operations in step 6, we obtain that the overall algorithm complexity is $O(n^2)$.
